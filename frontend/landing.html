CURATED INQUIRIES

Answers for the ambitious.

Everything you need to know about taking the assessment and securing your award.

The Aspire scholarship assessment is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students across all disciplines and graduation years. There is no fee required to register or complete the assessment.

The assessment is timed (typically 60 minutes) and evaluated across aptitude, technical thinking, and hands-on coding challenges executed securely in isolated test environments.

Grading is atomic and calculated instantly upon submission. If you score 90% or above, a 100% scholarship is automatically generated with a verifiable scholarship claim code and digitally signed achievement certificate.

Aspire features military-grade offline resilience: all progress is saved locally in IndexedDB and automatically re-synchronized with the backend upon connection recovery.
