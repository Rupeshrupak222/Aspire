Assessment Integrity & Rules

The assessment must be completed in a single uninterrupted sitting. Tab-switching, copy-pasting, and window minimizing are continuously recorded by the behavioral proctoring engine.

Network Recovery Assistance

If your internet connection experiences momentary loss, do not close your browser tab. Your responses are safely stored in your browser's local cache and will automatically synchronize when your connection restores.
